# Contributing

Thank you for your interest in Lyapunov.

**Ideas, bug reports and questions** are welcome as
[GitHub issues](https://github.com/Chaos-Tic/Lyapunov/issues): describe your
desktop (distribution, KDE, GNOME, sway…), what you did, what you expected and
what happened. `lyapunov doctor` helps.

**Pull requests are not accepted for now.** Lyapunov is developed by Chaos-Tic,
who keeps the ability to choose and change the project's license. A code
contribution could only be considered with a written agreement that grants
Chaos-Tic the right to license it under any terms; open an issue first if you
want to propose one.

Security problems: see [SECURITY.md](SECURITY.md).
